# Dungeon Draft

A roguelite deckbuilder. Fifteen floors, one fight per floor. Draft a card after every win, collect relics, rest or upgrade at campfires, and beat the bosses on floors 5, 10 and 15. Embers from each run buy permanent unlocks.

Play: https://saler-luka.github.io/dungeon-draft/

Controls: tap or click a card to play it (keys 1–0), E to end your turn.
